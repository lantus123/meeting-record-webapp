# Paper form mapping

The online form mirrors the supplied paper template:

- Department and meeting date
- Meeting types 1–16
- Joint meeting subtypes 5-1 to 5-3
- Teaching subtypes 9-1 to 9-3
- Topic, reporter, chairperson, time, location and participant count
- Discussion summary or full text
- Recorder and chairperson/director review
- A4 page (一) and continuation page (二)

The printed notes preserve the template guidance for clinical/pathology meetings and journal clubs.
